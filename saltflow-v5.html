Our Story

We're Just a Couple of High School Kids With a Truck and a Goal

SaltFlow started simple — we noticed our neighbors hauling heavy salt bags every month and thought, we could do that for them. So we did. What began as a way to earn extra money turned into a real local business we're genuinely proud of.

We're saving up for two big things: our church missions and college tuition. Every delivery you book goes directly toward those goals. You get soft water delivered to your door — we get one step closer to where we're headed.

We take this seriously. We show up when we say we will, we treat your home with respect, and we genuinely appreciate every single customer. Thank you for supporting local kids chasing something bigger.
